Ginger B Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Overview

Ginger B Games creates mobile games intended to be simple to use and respectful of player privacy. Our games are designed so that core gameplay does not require a Ginger B Games account.

Information our games use

Individual games may save gameplay information locally on your device, such as scores, achievements, unlocks, progress, preferences, audio settings, and similar game state. Local game data is used to make the game function and preserve your progress.

Personal information

Ginger B Games does not intentionally ask players to create an account or provide personal information simply to play our games. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Our games are intended to operate without third-party advertising or cross-app tracking unless a specific game's App Store listing states otherwise. If this changes for a future version, the App Store privacy disclosures and this policy will be updated as appropriate.

Apple services

Apple may process information when you download, purchase, update, review, or otherwise use an app through Apple services. Some games may also use Apple-provided features available through iOS. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Ginger B Games does not knowingly use its games to collect personal information directly from children. Players should not send sensitive personal information through support messages.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, or a description of the problem. We use that information only as reasonably necessary to respond to the support request.

Data retention

Local game data generally remains on the player's device unless the game, iOS, or the player removes it. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, or address follow-up issues.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Ginger B Games releases new titles or changes features. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are made.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact gingerbgames@gmail.com.
