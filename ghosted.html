About Ghosted

Ghosted is a single-player horror text-message game that turns your iPhone into part of the experience.

Conversations, haunting scenarios, choices, scares, and endings can vary from playthrough to playthrough. Your decisions affect how the story unfolds and whether you escape, survive the encounter, or allow the entity to get too close.

How to Play

Launch Ghosted, slide to unlock the simulated phone, and open the Ghosted app.

Read incoming messages and choose how you want to respond. Some decisions increase your protection while others may make the haunting more dangerous.

A full playthrough typically lasts approximately 8–12 minutes.

Random Conversations

Ghosted is designed for replayability. The game includes many different opening scenarios, haunting story arcs, dialogue variations, rare events, and branching decisions.

You may not receive the same conversation every time you play.

Achievements & Progress

Ghosted tracks achievements, discovered conversations, endings, haunting arcs, and other gameplay progress locally on your device.

Progress is intended to remain available between sessions unless the app is deleted or its local data is removed.

Optional Haunted Permissions

Camera Hauntings

Camera access is optional. If enabled, certain horror sequences may display the front camera and may temporarily capture an image for use inside the current fictional conversation.

Ghosted does not automatically save these images to your Photos library and does not upload them to Ginger B Games.

Location Hauntings

Location access is optional and is requested only while using the app. When enabled, Ghosted may use broad locality or city-level information as part of fictional horror dialogue.

Ghosted does not intentionally display your street address or precise coordinates in the game and does not transmit your location to Ginger B Games.

Microphone Hauntings

Microphone access is optional. When enabled, some sequences may detect whether sound is present nearby.

Ghosted does not transcribe your speech and does not retain microphone recordings.

Haunted Notifications

Notification access is optional. If enabled, Ghosted may occasionally deliver fictional horror notifications from the game.

Haunted notifications can be disabled from Ghosted's Settings screen or from iOS Settings.

Permissions Are Optional

Camera, microphone, location, notification, and wallpaper features are optional.

Declining a permission does not prevent you from completing the game, earning achievements, reaching endings, or experiencing the main Ghosted storyline.

Changing Permissions

If you previously denied a permission and want to enable it later:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and select Ghosted.
  3. Change the Camera, Microphone, Location, or Notification permission.
  4. Return to Ghosted.

Custom Wallpaper

Ghosted allows you to choose your own wallpaper for the simulated smartphone home screen.

The selected wallpaper is stored locally for use by the app and is not uploaded to Ginger B Games.

Accessibility & Comfort Settings

Ghosted includes settings intended to make the horror experience more comfortable for different players, including haptic controls and a Reduce Flashing option.

If you are sensitive to flashing effects or sudden scares, we recommend enabling Reduce Flashing before beginning a conversation.

Troubleshooting

The camera, microphone, or location feature is not working

Check iPhone Settings and confirm Ghosted has permission to use the requested feature. You can also open Ghosted's Settings screen and review Haunted Permissions.

I am not receiving haunted notifications

Make sure notifications are enabled for Ghosted in iOS Settings and that Real Haunted Notifications are enabled inside the game.

My wallpaper is not appearing correctly

Open Ghosted Settings, remove the current wallpaper, and select the image again.

My achievements or progress appear incorrect

Close and reopen Ghosted. The game automatically checks locally stored achievement progress when it launches.

The game does not fit correctly on my screen

Make sure you are running the latest available version of Ghosted and the latest compatible version of iOS. If the issue continues, contact support and include your iPhone model and iOS version.
