Haptic Hack

A tactile portrait safecracking game built around precision, haptic feedback, risk, guards, gear, achievements, and heist progression.

Need help?

If you're having a problem with Haptic Hack, first fully close and reopen the app and make sure you are running the latest version available from the App Store.

If the problem continues, contact Ginger B Games at gingerbgames@gmail.com.

When contacting support, please include:

  • Game name: Haptic Hack
  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • A short description of what happened
  • The screen or part of the game where it happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Common troubleshooting

The safe dial is not responding normally

Fully close Haptic Hack and reopen it. Make sure your finger begins directly on the safe dial when rotating it.

If touch behavior continues to seem unusual, restart your iPhone and try again.

Haptics are weak or not working

Open Settings inside Haptic Hack and check the Haptics setting.

Also make sure system haptics are enabled on your iPhone. Haptic feedback is an intentional part of the safecracking experience.

Music or sound effects are not playing

Open Settings and check the Music and SFX levels.

If audio remains unavailable, fully close and reopen Haptic Hack. Restarting the iPhone may also resolve audio that appears stuck.

My progress appears incorrect

Haptic Hack saves game progress locally on your device.

Fully close and reopen the game before taking further action.

Do not delete or reinstall Haptic Hack as an initial troubleshooting step. Deleting the app may also remove locally stored game progress.

If the problem continues, contact Ginger B Games before reinstalling and describe what progress appears to be missing.

Gameplay questions

What are Perks of the Day?

The Black Market offers a daily selection of perks and collectibles.

The selection remains the same throughout your local calendar day and refreshes on the next day.

You may own more than three functional perks, but a maximum of three perks can be equipped at one time.

What happens if I get BUSTED?

Getting BUSTED ends the current heist and causes you to lose the unbanked money from that run.

Previously banked money, owned gear, permanent statistics, and earned achievements remain saved.

Cash Out or Keep Going?

  • Cash Out safely adds the current run's winnings to your bank.
  • Keep Going continues the heist while risking the unbanked money from that run.

Does Haptic Hack require an internet connection?

No. Normal Haptic Hack gameplay is designed to work offline.

Does Haptic Hack require an account?

No. No account, sign-in, or registration is required.

Are there advertisements or in-app purchases?

No.

Haptic Hack contains:

  • No advertisements
  • No subscriptions
  • No in-app purchases
  • No real-money gambling
  • No purchasable virtual currency

All in-game money and equipment are earned through gameplay and have no real-world monetary value.

Privacy

Haptic Hack does not collect or transmit personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or gameplay tracking data.

Game progress, settings, equipment, statistics, and achievements are stored locally on the user's device.

See the Ginger B Games Privacy Policy for additional information.
