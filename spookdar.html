SpookDar

An augmented-reality ghost-hunting game that uses your iPhone as a paranormal detector and capture device.

AR & device help

  • Use SpookDar in a safe, well-lit area with enough room to move your phone comfortably.
  • If the AR view freezes, fully close and reopen the app.
  • Confirm Camera permission is enabled in iOS Settings if the AR hunt cannot start.
  • For indoor play, use the available in-home or limited-area option when appropriate.
  • Keep awareness of your real surroundings while following the radar or aiming at a ghost.

Need help?

If you're having a problem with SpookDar, first fully close and reopen the app and make sure you are running the latest version available to you.

If the problem continues, contact Ginger B Games at gingerbgames@gmail.com.

When contacting support, it helps to include:

  • Game name: SpookDar
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version, if known
  • A short description of what happened
  • The screen, mode, or feature where it happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Common troubleshooting

  • Fully close the app and reopen it.
  • Restart the iPhone if audio, haptics, camera, motion, or other device features appear stuck.
  • Check the app's Settings screen for relevant options.
  • If an update is available, install the newest App Store version.
  • If the issue continues, contact support before deleting the app if you want to preserve locally stored progress or settings.
